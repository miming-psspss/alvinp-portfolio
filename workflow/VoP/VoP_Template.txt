========================================================
  Variables of Prompting [VoP] — Precise Prompt Template
  Version 1.0 | Governance Framework Active
  Purpose:
  Reduce drift, hidden assumptions, and hallucinations
  by making key variables explicit.
========================================================

INSTRUCTIONS
  Copy this template at the start of any AI session.
  Fill in each variable for your specific task.
  Remove or mark [OPTIONAL] fields that do not apply.
  All variables marked [REQUIRED] must be defined.
  Refer to the Formal VoP Document for full definitions.

--------------------------------------------------------

  <Define how outputs will be verified before acceptance.>
